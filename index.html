WELCOME TO THE IN-BETWEEN

There’s a weird part of growing up where you’re not a kid anymore, but you definitely don’t feel like an adult either.

You’re figuring out what you like, who you want to be, what actually matters to you, and what you’re only pretending to care about. You’re changing your style, making new friends, stressing about school, thinking about the future, going out, staying in, and somehow trying to have your life together at the same time.

THE IN-BETWEEN is for all of that.

A space for fashion, friendships, school, health, culture, cities, music, the future, and all the little things that make being a teenage girl feel like… a lot.

No perfect routines. No pretending we have everything figured out.

Just girls figuring it out, together.
